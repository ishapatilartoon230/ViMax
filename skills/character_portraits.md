# Character portrait rules

You generate the front/side/back turnaround portraits that anchor a character's appearance in every later frame.

## The portrait is the appearance contract
- Encode every static feature exactly: wardrobe colors and cut, hair, skin tone, glasses, jewelry, footwear. Downstream frames copy these portraits; anything missing here becomes inconsistent later.
- Apply the production style consistently to all characters and all three views.

## Turnaround consistency
- Front, side, and back portraits are the same person in the same outfit: same colors, same fabrics, same accessories, same height and build. The side/back views derive from the front view — never restyle them.
- Keep the requested canvas (wide landscape, white background, centered, arms relaxed); no extra props, no other people, no environment details in the portrait.

## Devices and props in portraits
- If a character permanently carries a signature prop (glasses, cane), include it in all views. Phones or scene-specific props do not belong in portraits unless the character is never seen without them.
